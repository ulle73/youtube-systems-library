# Workflow

Steps 1–8 are directly supported by creator repository R2/R3; step 9 is an evaluation extension, not a demonstrated result.

1. Establish the audience and single intended takeaway. Select a reference for its communication job, not novelty alone.
2. Read the complete original prompt and its adaptation guide. Preserve source history; remove original-brand leftovers in the adapted draft.
3. Resolve a single timed film: scene content, transitions, exact copy, reading holds, audio and ending. Explain meaningful departures from the reference.
4. Choose only necessary assets. Distinguish identity, style, motion and actual source media. Storyboard panels are not automatically separate billable shots or literal opening frames.
5. Respect a requested prompt-review stop. Before authorized production, inspect real model schema, account access and read-only price information. Never generate merely to probe access.
6. Submit one complete film with bound asset identifiers, not local paths or unresolved placeholders. Save the exact request and job ID. If submission status is uncertain, check that job before resubmitting.
7. Inspect full playback and audio for timing, motion, content, identity, exact text and ending. Stills cannot establish smooth transitions or audio quality.
8. Within the approved budget, make at most one corrective full-film job or supported targeted edit by the source's default. Preserve the first output. Report remaining defects instead of entering an unlimited paid loop. Segmenting the film is a verified-constraint fallback, not the default failure response.
9. **INFERRED evaluation extension:** compare with the existing production route using accepted-output quality, active human minutes and all charged jobs. Include rejected attempts and finishing. Expand only after repeated useful output.

A failure does not prove that duration or shot count is unsupported. Inspect the error and actual schema before switching route. A technically completed export is not proof that the viewer understands the message.
