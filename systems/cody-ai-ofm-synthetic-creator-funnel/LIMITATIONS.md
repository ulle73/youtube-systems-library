# Limitations

## Source quality
This is a concrete tutorial but a weak source for business-performance claims.

Major penalties:
- strong course/affiliate/referral incentives;
- repeated large-income claims without auditable cohort data;
- self-reported screenshots/anecdotes;
- unsupported social-algorithm claims;
- auto-caption errors in product names;
- no independent reproduction of the complete workflow.

## Consent and likeness
The source demonstrates starting from a real person's online image. Current Fanvue help material requires authorization/rights and restricts AI media based on real people. This repository therefore substitutes owned/licensed references and does not preserve the original tactic as an implementation step.

## Disclosure and deception
The source repeatedly optimizes for a persona that feels real and human-like. Current Fanvue guidance requires AI disclosure and visible AI labeling. Any implementation must preserve transparency rather than imitate a real undisclosed person.

## Age-sensitive context
The source uses an age-18 persona example within an adult monetization workflow. This repository does not operationalize age-adjacent positioning or sexualized youth framing.

## Platform-evasion claims
The video's device/network/account warm-up and anti-detection guidance is not included. It lacks controlled evidence, can conflict with platform rules, and is unnecessary to reconstruct the safe funnel architecture.

## Revenue claims
Claims such as $92k in under a month, easy six-figure months, rapid $1k spenders, or 10-20x credit returns should be treated as promotional claims only.

## What remains unknown
- independent conversion and retention benchmarks;
- causal advantage of a synthetic persona over conventional creative;
- true cost per accepted asset after rejects/edits;
- privacy and reliability of conversational tooling;
- platform-policy stability;
- consumer trust impact of explicit AI disclosure;
- whether the source's content grammar improves purchase conversion rather than only engagement.
