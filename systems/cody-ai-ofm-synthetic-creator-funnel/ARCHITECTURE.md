# Architecture

## 1. Audience and persona layer
A single audience hypothesis and stable persona/brand specification controls tone, appearance, recurring themes and content boundaries.

**Source basis:** EXACT at approx. 03:00-04:30.

## 2. Rights and disclosure layer
Before generation, references must be owned/licensed and any synthetic identity must be disclosed according to the destination platform's current rules.

**Status:** RECONSTRUCTED compliance boundary. The source itself recommends a real-person reference workflow that current Fanvue guidance can conflict with.

## 3. Asset-production layer
A persistent character specification feeds batch image generation. Selected images can be animated into short videos. Production is treated as a repeatable workflow rather than one-off prompting.

**Source basis:** EXACT at approx. 05:56-10:30.

## 4. Creative-format layer
Short-form media uses a repeatable grammar:
hook → buildup → trigger → reaction → CTA.

**Source basis:** EXACT at approx. 13:44-15:10.

## 5. Distribution and attribution layer
Content drives to a measurable destination. Channel-specific tracking is configured before scaling.

**Source basis:** EXACT at approx. 16:50-18:00.

## 6. Conversation layer
Low-risk onboarding and context collection can be automated, but automation should be disclosed and bounded. Human handoff remains available.

**Source basis:** source demonstrates persistent chatbot persona and human handoff; transparent-AI constraint is RECONSTRUCTED from current platform rules.

## 7. Measurement layer
Evaluate downstream conversion, repeat spend/retention, total cost and human intervention rather than views or generation volume alone.

**Status:** RECONSTRUCTED/INFERRED from the source's retention emphasis and general workflow-design principles.

## Boundary
The source also contains a device/network/account anti-detection subsystem. It is not part of this architecture. It is unsupported as a causal growth mechanism and is intentionally excluded from reusable instructions.
